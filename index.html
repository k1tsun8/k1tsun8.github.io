Okay, but what is this site, really?

Welcome to the IWS. You'll note that there is in fact no such organization in our consenus reality. This is an interactive writing project inspired by popular internet-based psychological horror combined with a youthful fascination with natural disasters. All content on this website is strictly fictional. Do not attempt to use any information here during actual disasters. Any and all references to actual people, events, and places are entirely coincidental. Have fun, and let me know what you think!
