Global Hubium Safety Standards (GHSS)

The Global Hubium Safety Standards (GHSS) are a global set of guidelines first published in 2040 by the Global Hubium Safety Coalition (GHSC), updated/monitored yearly since as discoveries about the environmental impacts of Hubium were made.

The GHSC is responsible for enforcing these guidelines with the support of local governments if available.

GHSS-22: Requires that radiation detectors (e.g. Geiger counters) be shielded against low-to-moderate levels of Hubium Radiation (HR) to prevent damage to the sensor. All radiation detectors produced after 2053 are required to comply, or they cannot be sold legally.
